## About this check

- The certificate number is on the lower part of the certificate. You can type it with or without dashes.
- If the certificate has a QR code, scanning it opens this page and runs the check for you.
- A certificate marked **revoked** is no longer valid.

Think a certificate is fake, or found a mistake on yours? Contact Unit7000 and include the certificate number.
